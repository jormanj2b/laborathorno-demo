1. Who we are

This site belongs to Hearth & Crumb (Hearth & Crumb Bakery LLC, sample, replace), 8200 NW Example Ave, Doral, FL 33166 (fictional address). Privacy questions and requests: WhatsApp (555) 014-2290 (demo number) or hello@example.com (sample, replace).

2. What we collect and how

This site has no forms that send data to our server, creates no accounts, and uses no Google Analytics or ad pixels. We count visits with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no identifiers (see the cookie policy).

When you build an order in the catalog, what you type (name, preferred date, pickup or delivery, notes and items) stays in your browser’s localStorage, so the order isn’t lost when you change pages. We don’t receive any of it until you tap “Send order on WhatsApp” and send the message from your own account.

When you message us, we receive your phone number, the name on your account and whatever you write (items, quantities, date, delivery instructions).

3. How we use it

  • To answer questions and to confirm, bake and hand over your order.
  • To arrange payment, pickup or delivery.
  • To handle complaints or follow-up requests.

We don’t sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

4. Cookies, local storage and third parties

  • Your cart and your cookie choice are saved in your browser’s localStorage. The site needs them to work.
  • The Google Map loads only if you accept it in the cookie notice. Google may set its own cookies.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles any message you send us, under its own privacy policy.
  • Fonts are served from this site, with no connection to Google Fonts.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order messages only as long as we need them to fill the order and for tax records. You can delete what’s stored in your browser with “Clear order” or by clearing site data.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting WhatsApp (555) 014-2290 (demo number) or hello@example.com (sample, replace). Depending on where you live, state law may give you more rights, and we’ll honor them.

7. Children

This site isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with its last-updated date.
