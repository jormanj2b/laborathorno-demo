2. What data we handle and how we get it

This site has no forms that send data to its own server, creates no user accounts, and uses no Google Analytics or ad pixels. We count visits with GoatCounter, an analytics tool with no cookies or identifiers (see the cookie policy).

When you build an order in the catalog, what you type (name, preferred date, delivery method, notes and items) stays in your browser’s localStorage, so the order isn’t lost when you change pages. Laborathorno doesn’t receive any of it until you tap “Send order on WhatsApp” and send the message from your own account.

When you message us on WhatsApp, we handle your phone number, the name on your account and the details of your question or order (items, quantities, date, delivery instructions and anything else you write).

3. What we use it for

  • Answering your questions and handling, confirming and delivering your orders.
  • Arranging payment, pickup or delivery.
  • Handling complaints or later requests.

We don’t sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

4. Legal basis

We handle your data with your consent and to fulfill the order or question you send us, under the rights to data protection and informational self-determination in articles 28 and 60 of the Constitution of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela, and where applicable the Special Law Against Computer Crimes and the Law on Data Messages and Electronic Signatures.

5. Cookies, local storage and third-party services

The cookie policy details what this site stores in your browser and which third-party cookies may appear. In short:

  • Your order cart and cookie choice are saved in your browser’s localStorage. The site needs them to work as expected.
  • The Google Map loads only if you accept it in the cookie notice. Google may set its own cookies. If you reject it or haven’t chosen yet, the interactive map stays off; you can open the location in Google Maps in a new tab or accept later.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) is involved when you send or receive a message. It has its own policy.
  • Fonts are served from this site, with no connection to Google Fonts.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order data only as long as we need it to handle the order and meet legal or tax obligations. You can delete what’s stored in your browser with “Clear order” or by clearing site data. You can change your cookie consent from the cookie policy.
