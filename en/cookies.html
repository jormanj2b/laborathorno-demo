2. What we use in your browser

This site sets no tracking cookies of its own. We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies or identifiers. The site does use local storage and, only if you accept it, a Google Map that may set third-party cookies.

Essential storage (always on)

  • Order (hearthcrumb-order-v1 in localStorage): keeps your cart and order details (name, date, pickup or delivery, notes) on your device so nothing is lost when you change pages. We don’t receive it until you send the WhatsApp message.
  • Your choice (ck-hearthcrumb-demo in localStorage): remembers which categories you accepted and when. It lasts 12 months, then we ask again.

The catalog and the cookie notice need these to work. They’re never used for advertising.

Analytics

We count visits in aggregate with GoatCounter. It sets no cookies and stores no identifier on your device.

Marketing and third-party content: Google Maps (only if you accept)

If you accept this category (with “Accept all” or in “Settings”), we load an interactive Google Map of our area in Doral. Google serves that content and may use its own cookies, covered by Google’s privacy policy and its cookie information.

If you reject it or haven’t chosen yet, the map doesn’t load. You can still open the location in Google Maps in a new tab, or accept later.

3. How to change your choice

On your first visit you’ll see a notice with three equal options: Accept all, Reject all and Settings. Scrolling doesn’t count as accepting. You can change your choice any time with this button. Your saved order isn’t deleted.

4. How to delete this site’s data

Empty your order with “Clear order” in the cart, or clear site data in your browser settings.

5. Changes

If the way we use cookies or local storage changes, we’ll update this page and its date.
