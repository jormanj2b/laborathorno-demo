2. What we use in your browser

This site sets no first-party tracking cookies. We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies or identifiers. The site does use local storage and, only if you accept it, a Google Map that may set third-party cookies.

Essential storage (always on)

  • Order (laborathorno-order-en-v1 in localStorage): keeps your cart, quantities and order details (name, date, delivery and notes) on your device so nothing is lost when you change pages. Laborathorno doesn’t receive it until you send the WhatsApp message.
  • Your choice (ck-laborathorno-demo in localStorage): remembers which categories you accepted and when. It lasts 12 months, then we ask again.

The catalog and the cookie notice need these to work. They’re never used for advertising.

Analytics

We count visits in aggregate with GoatCounter. It sets no cookies and stores no identifier on your device, so it doesn’t need your consent in the notice.

Marketing and third-party content: Google Maps (only if you accept)

If you accept this category (with “Accept all” or by switching it on in “Settings”), we load an interactive Google Map showing our location in Chacao. Google serves that content and may use its own cookies and similar technologies, covered by Google’s privacy policy and its cookie information.

If you reject it or haven’t chosen yet, the interactive map doesn’t load. You can still see the location with the “Open in Google Maps” link (it opens a new tab), or accept later from the map box or the button below.

3. How to change your choice

On your first visit you’ll see a notice with three equal options: Accept all, Reject all and Settings (choose by category: essential, always on; analytics; marketing and third-party content). Scrolling on doesn’t count as accepting. You can change your choice any time with this button or the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer. Your saved order isn’t deleted.

4. How to delete this site’s data from your browser

Besides “Cookie preferences”, you can empty your order with the “Clear order” button in the cart, or clear site data in your browser settings (local storage for this address).

5. Changes

If the way we use cookies or local storage changes, we’ll update this page and its last-reviewed date.
