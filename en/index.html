Our story

Who we are

At Hearth & Crumb we believe in things made by hand.

We’re a neighborhood bakery in Doral, Florida, baking Venezuelan favorites and everyday classics the slow, honest way.

We don’t rush. We’d rather get it right.

Every item that leaves our kitchen carries a bit of us: patience, technique and that homemade touch. We’re a team that loves the work, and you can taste it.

Why choose us

What sets us apart

  • Handmade, start to finish

    We shape every piece by hand and give each dough the time it needs.

  • Quality ingredients

    We pick ingredients you can taste and feel in the texture.

  • Consistency

    The same result at 10 units or 1,000, order after order.

  • Flexibility

    We adjust flavors, sizes and packaging to fit your order.

  • Transparency

    Clear prices, honest lead times and direct contact with us.
